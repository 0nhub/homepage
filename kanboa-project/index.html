A Kanban board and conversation in the same place.

Kanboa is a browser workspace. Work lives on cards on a board. Talk about that work lives on the card: chat, notes, files, a checklist, pins, and a change log. Talk that is not about one card goes in a channel or a direct message.

The same cards can be shown as a board, a list, or a calendar. A filter hides cards that do not match; it does not copy them. The app is local-first in this repository (Vite/React, data in the browser). Sign-in, invites, and paid limits are part of the product model.

Who it is for

A small product or ops team that does not want a board in one app and chat in another.

Typical use:

  • Create a board (for example Product or Website).
  • Put work on cards; assign people and dates.
  • Discuss, attach files, and check off items on that card.
  • Use a channel for team talk that has no card yet.
  • Open Inbox for mentions and assignments; use list or calendar when you need a table or a week view.

A single person can use it without invites. Guests are for people who should see one board, not the whole workspace.

What it includes

Boards

Columns are status. Drag cards between them.

Cards

Title, people, date, tags, and custom fields.

Card modules

Chat, notes, one checklist, files, pins, and an activity log.

Channels and DMs

Team talk that is not attached to a card.

Inbox

Mentions, DMs, assignments, and automation notices.

Navigation

Search, Inbox, and pins first; then the rest by last inbound activity.

Views

Board, list, and calendar on the same cards.

Filter

People, labels, dates, and status. Matching cards stay; the rest are hidden.

People

Invite and roles: owner, admin, editor, viewer, guest. Org member list.

Sharing

Public board links. Guests on higher plans.

Also in the model

Automations, reminders, Trello import, and workspace settings.
