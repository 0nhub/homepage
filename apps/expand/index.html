Expand

Coming soon

An open canvas.
Boards you can go into.

Expand is a spatial board for macOS. Place text, images, files, and shapes on an open canvas, then nest boards you can actually go into.

Double-click the board to add something. Right-click to choose text, images, files, or shapes. The plus in the toolbar adds from the top of the window. Drag to arrange. Open a board to work inside it, then use the title-bar breadcrumbs to go back up.

Your boards stay on your Mac and, if you choose, in your iCloud Drive. Expand does not collect account data or analytics.

Build in

Unlimited nesting

Build as many sub-element layers as you need and go into each board.

Images and shapes

Place pictures and shapes on the same open canvas as your notes.

Kanban elements

Drop Kanban-style boards onto the canvas when a topic needs columns.

Mindmap lines

Connect elements with lines, the way you would on a mindmap.

Full-text search

Find text across your boards without hunting through folders.

Sub-navigation

Move through nested boards and return with title-bar breadcrumbs.

iCloud

Documents save as .exd packages, including on iCloud Drive if you choose.

Rich text

Write with bold, italic, and underline — not just plain notes.

Files on the board

Put PDFs and other files next to text, images, and sketches.
