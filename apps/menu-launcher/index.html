One Click Away

Menu Launcher is a focused macOS menu bar launcher for the apps and folders you use most. Add your preferred items once, then open them immediately without searching through Finder, the Dock, or application lists.

A left-click opens your personal launcher. Select an entry to launch an app or open a folder right away. A right-click gives you quick access to Settings or Quit.
