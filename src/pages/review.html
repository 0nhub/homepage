FAQ

What do you need to send?

Your URL. You can optionally send a short brief or specific tasks (e.g. “Try to complete signup” or “Test the dashboard”), or we go in completely blind and explore like a first-time visitor.

How long is the recording?

As long as it takes to cover your product properly — typically a thorough walkthrough from landing to signup and main features. The goal is depth, not a fixed duration.

What does the iOS add-on include?

The same style of test — screen recording and think-aloud feedback — run on iPad and/or iPhone (via macOS/Safari). Recommended if your users are on Apple devices.

My product is B2B or complex. Is that a fit?

Yes. Even complex tools need to make sense to new users. If a first-time user can’t figure out flows without a manual, your real users might struggle too. Tests are done with a first-time mindset.

When will I receive the video?

Delivery is typically within a few days. You’ll receive a private link to the recording plus a short written summary of the main findings.
