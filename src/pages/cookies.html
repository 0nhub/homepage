1. Summary

This website does not use cookies. The website code does not set cookies, does not use browser storage, and does not load tracking, analytics, or advertising scripts. That is why there is no cookie banner on this site.

2. Browser-side data

Your browser may store technical data on its own, for example in its cache, so that pages, images, and other content load correctly and faster. This happens on the browser side, is controlled by your browser, and serves only to load and display the content. It is not used by this website to recognise or track you.

3. What is not used

To be clear, none of the following is used on this website:

  • No tracking or analytics cookies and no other tracking technologies.
  • No cookies for advertising, marketing, retargeting, or profiling.
  • No cookies that give third parties access to your data or your browsing behaviour.
  • No social media plugins, advertising networks, or third-party fonts.

4. Embedded forms

The contact and newsletter forms are provided by the form service Deftform and are loaded from its servers when you open a page containing a form. No tracking or advertising functions are used for these forms. Any technically necessary processing by the provider to display and submit a form is governed by the Deftform privacy policy.

5. Manage cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies and other site data at any time in the settings of your browser. The browser vendors explain how in their official help pages:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari on Mac
  • Safari on iPhone and iPad
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Opera
