TopFold is a native macOS menu bar app with no Dock icon. It pins one to five real folders to the top edge of your screen and makes them usable like a Finder window, without opening Finder.

Move the pointer to the top and scroll down to open the overlay. It spans the full screen width, grows with its content, and stops at about 42 percent of the screen. Each folder gets its own column of equal width. These are real Finder folders through security-scoped bookmarks, not a separate file format, and a folder watcher keeps changes in sync live.

Files you drag in or out are always moved, the way Finder does it. A drop is never a copy. Dragging a file to the top edge opens the overlay and drops it into the folder under the pointer. Scrolling up at the top edge closes it again, as does a click outside, and Escape closes Quick Look first and the fold second.
