BDayMap keeps birthdays and reminds you in time. Add your own birthday as day and month only. Add other people with a name, photo, and date. The year is optional.

For each person you set reminders: on the day, one day before, three days before, one week before, or a custom number of days, each with a time. The Birthday widget shows one chosen person and a color on the Home Screen. The Upcoming birthdays widget shows the next four. A tap opens the app. The Apple Watch shows the same list, with your own birthday and no photo at the top.

iCloud sync is off and stays optional. When you turn it on, birthdays, photos, and reminders stay the same across devices with the same Apple ID. The app has no account of its own. On the Mac you can import birthdays from Contacts, only for contacts that already have a birthday. Name, date, and photo stay on the device. No ads. No purchases. No location. No map.
