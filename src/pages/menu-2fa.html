Menu 2FA is a focused macOS menu bar utility for two-factor authentication. Add your TOTP accounts once, then copy the current code with a single click—without opening a full authenticator app.

A left-click opens your accounts and copies a code. A right-click gives you Settings or Quit. Capture a setup QR code from your webcam or from a screenshot on your Mac.

Add accounts simply by dragging and dropping a QR image into the launcher.

Free positioning

Move your items freely whenever you want and arrange them exactly the way you like.

Minimalist by design

Keep the interface clean: settings stay hidden behind a secondary click.

Start at login

Open Menu 2FA automatically when you sign in to your Mac.

Unlock to view codes

Ask for authentication before codes become visible, so they stay protected until you unlock them.
