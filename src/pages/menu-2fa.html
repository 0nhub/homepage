Menu 2FA

Your codes,
one click away

Menu 2FA is a lightweight macOS menu bar utility for two-factor authentication. Store local TOTP accounts once, then copy the current 6-digit code with a single click—without opening a full authenticator app.

A left-click on the key opens your accounts and copies a code to the clipboard. A right-click gives you Settings, Launch at Login, or Quit. While the codes menu is open, the key becomes a countdown ring for the 30-second period.

Built for speed and quiet security

Menu 2FA keeps authenticator codes close at hand while staying out of your way.

One-click codes

Copy the current TOTP code for any account directly from the menu bar.

Local by design

Secrets stay on your Mac. Codes are generated on-device—no cloud sync and no developer analytics.

Clear countdown

While the menu is open, a ring shows how much of the 30-second window remains.

Simple setup

Add accounts with a title, optional icon, and Base32 secret—or paste an otpauth:// URI.
