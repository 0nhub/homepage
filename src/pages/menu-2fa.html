Two-factor authentication, without the detour.

Menu 2FA is a lightweight macOS menu bar utility for two-factor authentication. Store local TOTP accounts once, then copy the current 6-digit code with a single click—without opening a full authenticator app.

A left-click opens your accounts and copies a code to the clipboard. A right-click gives you Settings, Launch at Login, or Quit. While the codes menu is open, the key becomes a countdown ring for the 30-second period.
